What we focus on

Better business signals, not vanity activity.

Every project is different, so Beyond01 does not promise made-up percentages or one-size-fits-all outcomes. Instead, the work is organized around clear improvements that can be observed and measured.

Clarity
Can customers quickly understand what you sell, why it matters and what they should do next?
Discoverability
Can customers find the right products through navigation, search engines and campaign landing paths?
Conversion
Are avoidable questions, distractions or technical issues making it harder to complete a purchase or enquiry?
Measurement
Can you tell which channels, pages and customer actions are contributing to meaningful business outcomes?
Iteration
Do you have a practical list of what to test or improve next, based on evidence instead of random changes?

FAQ

Common questions before starting.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Shopify is a core specialization and the main platform focus. Digital marketing and SEO work can also support businesses whose website needs are broader, but e-commerce and Shopify are at the centre of Beyond01’s service offering.

Can you improve an existing Shopify store instead of rebuilding it?

Yes. A full redesign is not always the right answer. Existing stores can often be improved through targeted work on navigation, theme sections, product pages, speed, apps, SEO, tracking and conversion friction.

Can you help with both the website and advertising?

Yes. One of the main reasons Beyond01 combines Shopify and digital marketing is that the two affect each other. Better campaigns cannot fully compensate for a weak landing experience, and a strong store still needs qualified traffic.

Do you offer SEO for Vancouver businesses?

Yes. SEO work can include technical improvements, page structure, collection and service-page optimization, local relevance, on-page content and search-intent planning. The exact scope depends on the site and business model.

Do you work outside Vancouver?

Yes. Beyond01 is positioned in Vancouver and Metro Vancouver, while Shopify, SEO and digital marketing projects can also be delivered remotely for businesses elsewhere in Canada.

How does a project start?

Start by sharing what you sell, what you are trying to improve and what is currently not working. From there, the project can be scoped around the actual problem rather than forcing the business into a fixed package.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support can focus on testing, merchandising, campaign improvement, SEO, tracking and incremental Shopify changes. It can also be project-based if you only need help with a specific priority.
